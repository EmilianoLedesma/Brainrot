# The years we remember

An English Grounded Data Storytelling film requested with **Claude Opus 5.5**.
Explicit model selection is task-local; no provider/API fallback. Brainrot's
storytelling skill and speech pipeline remain in use. Claude writes the story,
direction and film code; source is reviewed before local execution.

## Three structures

1. One equal-duration clock interval opens into a memory landscape, then returns
   to real research which limits a too-simple story of aging.
2. Start with participant counts from three studies, then explain the memory
   hypothesis. This risks making a literature review instead of a human story.
3. Draw a universal age-versus-felt-speed curve. Reject: no suitable individual
   dataset or verified age-group means is available, and this would invent data.

Choose the first: the felt year is a retrospective judgement, not faster physical
time. One physical red–blue pencil and one paper world carry the film. The script
should preserve the user's idea as a plausible mechanism, with proportionate
attention to conflicting evidence and alternative explanations.

## Honest data

The quantitative anchors are sourced study sample sizes, verified age ranges
and sampling design. A 2005 survey had N=499 ages 14–94. A 2021 paper examined
samples 293,263,262, not three measurements of memory quantity. A 2026 study had
120 adults in six equal groups of 20, an explicitly designed sample. Neither
sample counts nor a balanced sampling chart is a time-speed curve or proof.
Memory-landmark drawings are qualitative and labelled illustration. No measured
memory counts, fabricated scatterplot, invented brain scan or causal percentage.

## Style and editing

Data Storytelling's graphite marks, traceable values, acting red–blue pencil,
pinned notes, camera pushes/pulls/tracks and chart-operation morphs. One take
over cream paper. No progress bar, fixed series header, slideshow or quote card.
Spoken captions in a separate bottom figure-caption region. Notes move with
their marks. Use a limited palette so empirical records and illustrative notes
remain distinguishable. Sonification follows real chart values; qualitative
scenes use ordinary foley rather than inventing a measurable pitch scale.

## Review

Review generated JavaScript as source, render real style frames, check text
visibility/reading time, inspect final timeline/action frames, fully decode and
measure audio/video. Run normal-speed browser playback; do not equate that with
a sensory watch/listen. No database job, worker or upload.
