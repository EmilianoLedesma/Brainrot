# Data and scientific limits

The film distinguishes retrospective life-tempo judgments from experiencing a
minute in the present. Memory landmarks and routine are a plausible account,
not a complete established cause of age differences. All memory drawings are
qualitative illustrations; no fabricated individual observations or age curve.

The small data table is [dataset.json](dataset.json). Its numbers are factual
sample/design counts, not measurements of memory quantity or felt time speed.

| Record | Verified quantity | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Wittmann & Lehnhoff 2005](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16512313/) | 499 respondents, ages 14–94 | A survey supporting small/moderate age associations; no invented age-specific means. |
| [Kosak & Hilbert 2021](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.744551/full) | Three samples: 293, 263, 262 | Numbers of reported events/memories did not predict passage-of-time judgments in those analyses. Sample sizes are not event counts. |
| [Teghil et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.3758/s13421-026-01879-1) | 120 adults, six recruited age groups, 20 per group | Observational recruitment design, not a population distribution or a measured age trend. Last group ages 70–91. New-information encoding association does not establish causation. |
| [Life-tempo review](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2025.1747171/full) | No quantitative series copied | Distinct retrospective phenomenon; no settled unified mechanism. |

Published aggregate facts are restated in an original visual encoding; no paper
figure, participant-level dataset or copyrighted illustration is copied. Source
credit is retained in the local project. The 2021 Frontiers paper and 2026 study
are open-access research; their specific asset/dataset licenses remain applicable
to any further reuse beyond these cited factual counts.

Equal-width calendar units denote equal clock duration. Any expansion or
contraction above them is labelled illustration and carries no invented numerical
memory scale. The ending offers enriched recollection, not a promise of slowing
physical time, reversing aging or a clinical intervention.
