# Coffee, from seed to cup

An unpublished comparison in English: the same original Brainrot Grounded script,
spoken title, voice, pauses and exact audio file in both 1080 × 1920 films.
Duration comes from measured speech, between 90 and 180 seconds. No platform upload.

## Three possible structures

1. Follow a seed through roasting, grinding and brewing, then explain why changing
   one brewing variable changes the cup.
2. Begin with two cups made from the same beans; reveal the extraction mechanism,
   uneven flow, then return to the cups with a practical distinction.
3. Dive inside one ground particle, follow water out, and pull back to the brewer.

Choose the second structure if supported by the generated script, using the first
as its causal spine and the third as its signature close-up. The exact spoken
structure remains the output of Brainrot's existing storytelling code, not a
manually substituted screenplay. Shot boundaries are then generated with Codex
using the Lemo directing/style guides and mapped to the existing TTS words.

## Visual direction

Adapt Lemo's Isometric Infographic grammar: a cream tabletop diorama, fixed
30-degree projection, three-tone solids, leader pins, color-coded material and
water, nested cutaways. Keep one seed/particle identity through the explanation.
Use original geometry and coffee-specific staging; no demo story or assets.

Learn from Stupy Doo's previously reviewed reference: subject continuity, visual
explanations, transformations and varied scale. From Lemo's style guide: a
continuous world, process routes, cutaways and labels that belong to the scene.
Do not copy characters, specific shots, artwork or music from either source.

## Shot vocabulary and timing

The generated shot plan uses these topic-specific scenes: two cups, green seed,
roast chemistry, grinding/surface area, porous-particle extraction, brewing
controls, uneven-flow cutaway, concentration versus extraction, final return.
Every shot has an exact spoken cue, a camera instruction, short labels, a reason
for the view and a takeaway. Audio word times are authoritative; no invented
study results or numerical yield graphics. Particle/flow drawings are schematic.

Opening: cups appear before the title finishes. Camera pushes into a coffee seed,
follows matter through the process, changes scale for dissolved material, then
returns to the cups. Introduce labels only when their concepts are spoken.
Keep a clear subtitle region and at least a third of the frame for the main prop.

## Sound and comparison controls

Baseline: untouched production Remotion composition, device-selection guidance,
speech and mastering. Lemo version: same narration plus quiet procedural pour,
grinding and accent sounds from the vendored MIT sound library. Preserve voice
priority and Brainrot's −16 LUFS / −1.5 dBTP target. A shared-audio-only Lemo
version is also exported so sound design can be judged separately.

## Review

Render representative frames before the whole film; check key transitions at
0.2-second intervals, text metadata with the upstream reading-time checker,
complete decode, duration, codecs, black frames, loudness and speech preservation.
Record honestly whether full-speed audiovisual review was possible; contact
sheets and numerical checks alone do not establish production quality.
