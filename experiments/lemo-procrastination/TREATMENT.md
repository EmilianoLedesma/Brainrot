# Why starting feels harder

An original English Grounded short about procrastination as short-term emotion
regulation. Brainrot generates the spoken script; Codex independently compares
three Lemo library styles and selects the best fit. Its saved choice and later
shot plan are evidence of that decision. The style is not inherited from coffee.

## Three story structures

1. A worker approaches a blank document, escapes into a phone, then approaches
   the same document differently. A concrete loop with an earned callback.
2. Present and future selves pass the same task between them, revealing how
   immediate relief can produce later pressure.
3. Travel inside an abstract control room of emotional priorities, then return
   to the task. This risks implying a literal brain circuit, so avoid it.

Choose the first, borrowing the temporal contrast of the second only where the
generated narration supports it. The third is rejected as a literal explanation.
All character/actions are original illustrations, not clinical evidence.

## Editing brief

One subject, a clear goal and an emotional obstacle. The same document, phone
and protagonist carry continuity. No progress bar, no fixed top header, no
chapter headings or slide frames. Brief diegetic words belong on props; bottom
word-timed captions carry the complete narration. Open with action, not a logo.

Use real staging: anticipation, reach, retreat, relief, renewed pressure and
the first small action. Camera must reveal information: push into a hesitation,
track a reaching hand, pan with a distraction, pull out to show the delayed task,
match shapes between the phone and the task, settle for the final first line.
Foreground/midground/background respond differently to camera moves. Use an
object crossing the lens, a paper unfolding and circular match cuts to link
scenes. Keep the camera level enough that text can be read. Effects should
support the mechanism, not cover the narrator or introduce invented facts.

## Benchmarks and boundaries

Learn continuity and explanatory visual changes from the Stupy Doo references
previously reviewed in this repository; learn camera/performance/self-checks
from Lemo DIRECTOR.md. No copied artwork, character, shot, music or demo story.
The actual selected STYLE.md supplies material/palette/motion grammar.

## Sound

Voice remains primary. A quiet original synthesized motif marks the avoidance
loop; paper/phone/tap sounds sit on visible actions. Use short near-silent
intervals around the turning point. Keep Brainrot's −16 LUFS export target and
90–180-second measured speech range. No paid media or voice-cloning service.

## Review and deliver

Inspect real rendered style frames before full render; then inspect final-file
timeline sheets and 0.2-second strips of key transitions. Run upstream text
reading-time/bounds checks, complete media decode, black-frame/loudness checks
and actual browser playback. Distinguish these from a sensory watch/listen.
Deliver one final MP4, SRT, poster, source, saved narration/timing/plan and credits.
